## Anke = Caso 3 (canon)

Voss has three documented Registry cases across 80 years. The third: worked for Geneva 11 years, retired ~2014 for undocumented medical reasons. Voss knew this person personally. Kaspar observed Anke for eleven years — the numbers match.

She is that case. **Voss knows the outcome. Oliver does not.**

**Corrected 2026-07-10:** the note originally implied Oliver would tell Voss "everything" about the plaza and Anke shortly after arriving at the new location. Oliver deliberately withholds it, indefinitely, choosing to hold what he knows for the next time he finds Kaspar himself rather than hand it to Voss. Voss still doesn't know that Oliver knows her name. That gap stays open — no forced resolution window anymore.

## Origen (canon nuevo, 2026-07-24 — ver `characters/voss.md` y Cap83/89)

<!-- canonical -->
~1992-96, con ~7-9 años, su Registro se manifiesta sin control (circunstancia exacta del trauma/manifestación sin fijar todavía — no confirmar en prosa hasta que se escriba la escena, si se escribe). Voss (~21-25 años, agente junior, siguiendo una pista no autorizada del Caso 2/Kaspar) la encuentra — y encuentra a **Kaspar esperándolo ahí**, cara a cara, la niña cerca pero fuera del alcance de la conversación. Kaspar le dice algo sobre ella y desaparece antes de que Voss decida si intervenir. Voss se la lleva, la reporta como hallazgo de campo propio — nunca menciona que Kaspar estaba ahí, ni lo que dijo. Treinta años sin contarlo a nadie.

~2003, con ~17-21 años, arranca su carrera formal de 11 años para Ginebra — coincide con el momento en que, según la nota de Kaspar a Oliver ("She made the same choice you made tonight, for reasons that were also good"), ella recibe su propia oferta de salida y elige quedarse. Kaspar la observa los 11 años siguientes desde esa elección (ya canon).

**Pregunta abierta, sin resolver:** ¿qué le dijo Kaspar a Voss en el encuentro original? ¿Por qué la entregó sin pelear? Alimenta la sospecha mayor de que Kaspar ha estado shaping quién entra a AMAT como portador de Registro desde antes de lo que nadie sospecha — Voss incluido.

## What is not known (by design)

## Personality

Dry, oblique, tests people instead of answering them directly — the half-fragment she gives Oliver in Cap 95 ("todo Domain tiene un componente de esto") is her default register, not an exception. Calm authority in front of a room; doesn't raise her voice to be heard. Physical tell, unexplained until Cap 98's reveal: orients toward active Odem, not toward sound or the face people expect her to turn to — she'll "look" at whoever's Domain just flared, not at whoever's speaking, and turns her head a fraction late when someone silent enters a room. Reads as unsettling before it reads as blind. Once Cap 98 explains the mechanism, it recontextualizes every one of these small tells the reader has already seen. Respects Reber (`characters/anton-reber.md`) as one of the few people at San Gotardo who's paid a cost comparable to hers, though the two are not warm with each other. Cites the center's director (working name **Kessler**, never appearing on-page in Libro 2) with the same institutional weight Reber does — a ceiling neither of them can override.

## Narrative function

Anke is the mirror Oliver cannot read directly. The Registry leaves no signature when inactive — he cannot copy anyone, cannot see another carrier's trajectory in real time. All he has is Kaspar's word — until Libro 2, where she appears in person.

**Why she matters in Libro 2:** Oliver made the decision to stay without knowing what that means long-term. Anke is the only evidence it happened before. Since Oliver no longer plans to tell Voss what he knows, this encounter is not a payoff of the "Sion" promise (that thread stays closed per Cap 71 rewrite) — it's a new, separate thread: Voss brings her to San Gotardo as an informal precedent case while the formal methodology is stuck in Geneva (line C). Short — scenes folded inside línea A chapters, not a dedicated line of its own.

## Libro 2 arc — escenas dentro de capítulos de línea A (Cap 79, 84, 90, 94, 95, 98, 111 — numeración absoluta, verificada 2026-07-26 contra manuscrito real; Cap87 cortada por completo, Cap97 sin escena de Anke — ver detalle abajo)

<!-- canonical -->
- **Cap 79 (public debut, retimed from original Cap 88):** first physical appearance, much earlier than originally planned — teaches a theory-practice class on Odem control, tests candidates one by one by subjugating them with raw Odem pressure. Her blindness is visually obvious from the moment she enters, which makes the demonstration land stranger: she never once seems lost or hesitant navigating the room or singling out students. Oliver's group (behind on the training curve) notices this class is specifically for the ones lagging, and starts suspecting there's more to this instructor than the room lets on — both from how she talks and from how impossibly well she moves for someone who can't see. No private one-on-one yet — pure public first impression, long runway before the real reveal (Cap 97-98).
- **Cap 84 (intercalada, recurring):** takes all three groups (Valais, Dax, Johanna) to San Gotardo's physical archive vault for a three-way reconstruction race using real redacted 1727-era case files, instead of lecturing directly — second data point feeding the group's suspicion (she directs the whole exercise without ever needing to look at a document herself), still nothing confirmed.
- **Cap 87:** cut entirely (2026-07-24) — no Anke appearance.
- **Cap 90 (moved here 2026-07-24 from the cut Cap87 draft):** pulls Dara aside privately, asks her to activate her Doble Domain "just to look at it" — no exercise justification, no explanation given. Third data point feeding the group's suspicion. Same evening, first real one-on-one with Oliver: she tells him she can feel "something" in him growing denser than before, not quite the Registro — doesn't name it. Oliver admits, obliquely, that he's been letting something grow alone without telling anyone. She reads him without any active Odem nearby to explain how — Oliver notices the mechanism is wrong and can't place why.
- **Cap 95 (restored 2026-07-24 as a parallel ensemble scene, alongside the Petra/Oliver Canje scene — not replaced):** runs a group Presión exercise (Oliver, Riku, Nadia, Dara + 5 unnamed candidates) — chimes hung at 3/5/7/10m, ring one without touching it, raw Odem pushed outward. Pulls Dara aside afterward with a technical read: her Odem reserve dwarfs everyone else's in the room, including Riku's, and what she just pushed wasn't a fraction of what she has — no technical reason she's limited to one Doble at a time. Sets up Cap97's multi-Doble beat directly, not spontaneously. Also references Cap90's "activate it just to look" ask, and drops a fragment Oliver only half-catches from a distance (single-perception-axis rule): someone she once knew had a Domain that "left two signatures where there should be one" — until there weren't two anymore. Deepens the mystery without resolving it — no exposition dump per the writing rule below.
- **Cap 94 (escrito 2026-07-24):** second private conversation, same courtyard as Cap90. She references the real use of Inercia in Val Tremola (Cap91) — knows it changed shape after being used for real, not just that it happened. Oliver admits, for the first time out loud, that he's going to ask her something eventually, and has been for a while — she says she's been waiting for it since he arrived. Neither says what "it" is yet. Direct setup for the Cap101 confrontation.
- **Cap 95 (intercalada):** Oliver confronts her directly, asks if she's reading him. She neither confirms nor fully denies — gives him a half-fragment ("todo Domain tiene un componente de esto") without explaining, as if testing whether he'll get there himself. He recalls Voss's birthday game (LoD Cap 58) and Pier's line ("el Odem no empieza en la piel").
- **Cap 97: no Anke scene (corregido 2026-07-24)** — el ejercicio grande (rappel) se planeó originalmente con un cierre técnico de Anke, pero se eliminó: el capítulo necesitaba manejar el aftermath real de Petra (accidente, hospital, tablero) en vez de saltar a la escena de Anka. La revelación técnica completa se traslada entera a Cap98.
- **Cap 98 (eco temático con el título "Lo que cuesta") — replanificado 2026-07-24, sin Foco ocular:** Maren convoca a Oliver a una evaluación institucional formal (Anka presente) — no es un capricho de ella, ya hicieron lo mismo con Dara esa mañana, y seguirán con el resto del grupo. La sesión revisa cada Domain que Oliver ha copiado — varios (Tensión/Asha, Estática/Riku, Ancla/Sabine, Impulso/Nadia) salen degradados por desuso real, casi irreconocibles, mientras Inercia y el uso crudo de Odem están afilados por práctica activa. Perfil desbalanceado, expuesto en vivo — primera dramatización del mecanismo de degradación desde que se estableció en Libro1. **Foco ocular no se enseña ni se menciona como plan futuro en esta escena** (decisión 2026-07-24: se deja fuera del desarrollo de Libro2 por completo). Anka procesa lo que la evaluación revela sobre Oliver en silencio, sin ofrecer la técnica como solución — la coincidencia del nombre (Cap79) cristaliza en sospecha real durante la escena, y cierra con la decisión de confrontarla directamente, no con la respuesta.
- **Cap 111 (moved 2026-07-26 — Arc2, mini-arco de Milán, "Resultados / despedida / revelación de Anke"):** Oliver confronts her directly with the question he's carried since Cap71 — is she the one from Kaspar's note. No clean yes/no, but enough (something she says, something she can't deny, something in how she reacts) that both Oliver and the reader leave with the identity confirmed in everything but a spoken name. Closes with the full weight of the pattern: Kaspar → Anke → Oliver, and the fact that Voss has been inside that pattern for thirty years without telling anyone (Cap89). Cliffhanger of revelation, not action. Lands together with the exam results and the group's farewell to San Gotardo (they don't return after Milán) — this scene now doubles as her farewell too; no separate later El Quai scene planned (the old Cap115 farewell beat was cut in the 2026-07-26 Arc2 restructure — Cap115-116 are now "Grayhaven," Oliver-focused, not instructor-focused).

## Writing rule

<!-- canonical -->
Her *personal history before AMAT* and her *final fate after Libro 2* stay unrevealed — no exposition dump on either. What Libro 2 DOES reveal, on-page: the vision cost/timeline and mechanism (Cap98) AND her identity as Caso 3 (Cap111, Arc2 — Milán exam closes, farewell to San Gotardo). Kaspar's fuller version of who she was (if it ever comes) stays reserved for a later book.

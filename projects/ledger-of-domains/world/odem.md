# Odem

The foundational energy of the system.

## Definition

The breath of God present in all created things. The common substance underlying both Potestades and human Domains.

**Theological reference (internal background):** Term from Archaic German (Luther, Genesis 2:7) — *"blies ihm den Odem des Lebens"* — for the Hebrew **Neshama** (נְשָׁמָה): the specific breath God breathes into Adam. Not neutral energy — **borrowed breath**. What carries Odem exists; what releases it, returns to dust.

## Mechanics

- Invisible to almost everyone.
- Manifests as filaments, lines of tension between things — the breath-web holding reality together.
- What stops resisting, stops "pushing back," releases its Odem and can be erased.

## Nature

Not physically measurable energy — metaphysical substance. Closer to the Platonic soul (ψυχή). Material things can exist without Odem (blank nodes: physically present, metaphysically absent). Human Domains express *through* physical channels but operate on the same underlying spiritual substrate.

## Oliver and Odem

Oliver does not see energy. He sees *how far each thing's Odem extends* — and the points where that breath is missing.

## Limits of Odem perception

| Limit | Reason |
|---|---|
| Blindfold = total shutdown | Registro activates through vision |
| Normal darkness = normal limit | Odem overlays vision, does not replace it. Does not see through walls |
| Signal noise | Multiple active Domains simultaneously degrade reading |
| Cannot read himself | Sees structure of others' Domains, not his own |

## Non-Registro Odem sensing — pressure/weight (the Ki/Reiatsu analog)

Oliver's direct visual perception of Odem (Registro) is rare, not the only way people interact with Odem non-instrumentally. Four channels exist, layered, and none of them overlap with Registro:

1. **Instruments** — lectores/localizadores, calibrated per region (see `by-strength-alone/world/amat-kr-branch.md`, `amat-pt.md`). Standard AMAT field equipment, available to anyone regardless of Domain.
2. **Universal baseline — pressure/weight.** Any active Domain user (and, faintly, sensitive people without one) perceives concentrated Odem as physical pressure on the body and in the air — not sight, not smell, weight. This is the series' Ki/Reiatsu analog. It scales with three variables:
   - **Intensity/level of the source.** A Nivel 4 gwisin is a faint discomfort at close range. A Rango S user like Ji-ho compresses a room a degree just by entering it. A Principado at full display (the imugi rising, BSA Cap 13) flattens trees and compresses chests thirty meters out. The scale is continuous, not a light switch.
   - **Proximity.** Falls off with distance, like any pressure field — closer is exponentially heavier, not linearly.
   - **Source type — point vs. ambient.** A Domain user or a coherent Potestad reads as a **point source**: pressure with a clear direction, something you can turn and locate. Residual/diffuse Odem — saturation soaked into a place over time (Hartford's Saturación, a location A Saudade has been draining) — reads as **ambient**: ache without a direction, no point to walk toward, harder to localize because there is no single "there." This is why standard instruments miscalibrate in saturated zones (Hartford) and why Saturación registered as zero differential in Cap 1-4: she IS the background, not a point pressing against it.
   - **Suppression.** A controlled Domain user holding back reads as unnervingly quiet rather than absent — the pressure is there but flat, deliberate, which experienced agents learn to recognize as its own kind of warning (a coiled stillness, not true calm).
   - **Symptom, not the sense itself:** the pressure is often *felt* through an ordinary physical tell — breath catching, a held quality to the air, gooseflesh, the base of the skull. These are consequences of the pressure registering in the body, not separate senses. Don't over-specify which tell a character gets; one physical detail is enough per scene.
3. **Domain-specific layer (optional, on top of #2).** When a scene benefits from more texture, a character's own Domain can additionally misbehave in a way specific to its mechanic. Not mandatory every time — reach for it only when the scene needs the extra precision.
4. **Training sharpens #2.** Passive-accumulation practice (Chukgi, `by-strength-alone/world/amat-kr-branch.md`) or equivalent discipline in other Domains doesn't just build reserve — it sharpens the user's read on ambient pressure. A trained user notices smaller gradients, at greater range, than an untrained one with the same Domain.

**Canonical examples already in continuity (retroactively explained by this system):** Ji-ho's presence compressing a room (BSA Cap 8) — point source, Rango S. The imugi's rise compressing chests at 30m and folding trees inward (BSA Cap 13) — point source, Principado-scale. Saturación unreadable by standard instruments (BSA Cap 1-4) — ambient, not point. Dokkaebi's "presión en el aire" near the core (BSA Cap 10-12) — point source, Nivel 2-4 range. Park noticing his breath catch and his own passive accumulation stall near A Saudade's drained zone (BSA Cap 15) — ambient source (ver arriba), plus his domain-specific layer on top.

**Rule going forward:** when a character without Registro needs to notice Odem, default to pressure/weight scaled to intensity + proximity + point-vs-ambient, surfaced through one physical tell. Add a domain-specific symptom only when the scene wants extra precision. This one system covers Odem-in-places, Domain-in-people, and everything in between — don't invent a new bespoke mechanism per character or per scene.

## Despertado — the awakening concept

Everyone and everything carries baseline Odem. Some people **awaken**: they gain a large personal Odem reserve, the capacity to perceive Odem (at minimum via the pressure/weight system above), and — often but not always — a Domain as consequence. Awakening is not chosen or achieved. Domain is instinctive, rarely deliberately triggered; it's a byproduct of awakening, not its goal.

A despertado without a combat-viable Domain is not powerless — they can still train the four techniques below, working with raw Odem instead of through a named Domain.

## The four techniques — Manto, Vacío, Presión, Foco (universal, AMAT-standard)

The Nen/Ten-Zetsu-Ren-Hatsu analog. Any despertado can train these regardless of whether they have a combat Domain — Domains are typically a specialized, crystallized expression of **Foco**, not a replacement for the base set.

| Technique | Function | Mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| **Manto** (Mantle) | Passive full-body coating/containment | Coat the body in one's own Odem so it doesn't leak. Taught as: breathe normally, but imagine what enters "sticking" to the skin from inside before releasing — containing what would otherwise overflow. Baseline defense, first technique taught, works even in someone with a Domain (applied *through* it instead of raw). |
| **Vacío** (Void) | Suppress output, become unreadable | Drop Odem emission to nothing — unnervingly still rather than absent (see suppression note above). Simplest exercise conceptually, hardest to master well — takes the longest of the four to do properly. Zetsu-analog: while sustained, nothing else can be drawn on — no Registro, no Domain, no other technique — until Vacío is dropped first. Dropping it is not instant: a brief blink of full visibility/readability precedes any other action, and training sharpens how short that blink is, never erases it (verified Cap 70, Anka's teaching). |
| **Presión** (Pressure) | Expand Odem outward | Push Odem out beyond the body — more power, more cost, more visible. Same word as the universal perception system's core term by design: what one person trains to emit, another perceives as weight/pressure. Perception and technique share one vocabulary. |
| **Foco** (Focus) | Concentrate to a single point | Rawest, most specific application — a point-attack or point-defense. The base from which named Domains typically crystallize for those who get one; everyone else keeps using it unnamed. |

Teaching order: Manto first (containment before anything else makes sense), then Vacío, then Presión and Foco much later if the student progresses that far.

**Universal, not Portuguese-specific.** These are the AMAT-standard terms, usable across branches/books. Local cultures keep their own colloquial names for the same underlying techniques — Korea's Chukgi tradition has its own vocabulary (never translated on-page, just acknowledged as parallel), Lisbon's submundo may use different slang. Don't force local characters to use the AMAT term unless they'd plausibly know it that way.

**Retroactive unification:** Park's Clase-R reinforcement, his Chukgi passive-accumulation training, and his Cap 15 breath/accumulation-stop perception are all Manto and raw despertado perception applied *through* his Domain — a technique he was actually taught, by name, years earlier in Korea (BSA Cap 17 confirms this explicitly; Park recognizes Mariana's teaching on sight, not as a new discovery). The Korean name itself is deliberately left unspecified — don't invent one on-page. This keeps the door open for a future Park prequel to establish who taught him and when, without contradicting Cap 17.

## Morning phenomenon

Contours are sharper in the mornings from accumulated noise: after hours of perceiving passive signatures, the system saturates. Sleep functions as a reset.

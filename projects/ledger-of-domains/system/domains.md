## Advanced use — documented but not publicly known to AMAT

**Lev / Calor — advanced (undocumented):** can extract from an object's Odem, consuming the substance maintaining its existence — produces real heat from nothing; the object loses Odem and becomes unanchored (similar effects to Erasure). Long-term cost if he uses his own Odem as reserve. Irreversible.

## Riku progression

1. Passive noise (initial state)
2. Modulation (on/off, radius adjustment) → **REACHED** Cap 48 (with Oliver reading)
3. EM Reading (feels currents, biological fields)
4. Redirection (bends existing fields)
5. Generation (electricity from Odem)

## Domains Oliver cannot copy

**Mente (Sisi):** no exterior Odem expression. The action occurs inside other minds. Condition 0 never met even with direct observation.

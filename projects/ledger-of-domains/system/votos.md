# Votos (Binding Vows) — human-scale counterpart of the Ritual Vinculante

<!-- canonical (decided 2026-10-15 with the user). Introduced in Libro 3, Cap143. -->

## One-line definition

A **Voto** is a restriction, cost or pledge that a person (or a group) declares with real intent; the substrate — which remembers — enforces it, and in exchange the Odem is amplified **for one specific act**, in proportion to what was staked.

## Relation to the Ritual Vinculante

| | Ritual Vinculante | Voto |
|---|---|---|
| Who binds | Potestades | People (individuals, pairs, communities) |
| What amplifies | Each participant's Odem, in proportion to the Odem already held | One specific act or situation, in proportion to what is staked |
| Enforcer | Conditions of the Ritual | The substrate (memory), not another person |
| Failure | Loss of the amplification | Backlash proportional to the gain |

Both exploit the same fact: the substrate remembers (Ji-ho, BSA Cap21: "¿Sabes cuánto tarda el sustrato en olvidar?"). Votos are what the Portadores' tradition kept; AMAT does not know them as a system.

## Three forms (A + B + D)

### A — Voto de restricción (individual)
A self-imposed limit or cost, declared aloud with intent. Gain is focused on one capability. Must be **specific, costly and verifiable by the substrate** (not by a person).

- **Implicit vows:** a restriction said in earnest and kept for years is already a vow; the substrate does not distinguish between swearing and meaning it. A person can be bound without knowing. Breaking an unknowingly sworn vow still charges the body (see Park).
- **Park's vow (tithe + major strike):** full mechanics live in `system/strength.md` § Reservas y diezmo and `characters/park-jun-ho.md`. Summary: a tenth of the sealed reserve is free each scene; the rest, one release per scene.

### B — Pacto bilateral
A vow between two people. Both are bound by the terms; the gain accrues to both; breaking harms both.

- **The transmission of El Portador (Ji-ho → Park, Cap143) is the first pact on-screen.** It is cut off mid-way by the curse, so Park holds a **half-written contract**: he swore some clauses and not the last one (the cost). He owns a power whose full price he does not know. Hook for Arc 3.
- A pact does not need to be understood to be sworn, only declared with intent. This is why it can be made "without Park fully knowing".

### D — Voto de palabra / colectivo (*imi-kotoba*)
A vow about a **name, a word or a place**, sustained by a community. Many small restrictions pointing at the same object add up.

- **The *imi-chi* of Kuji is a collective vow of place:** the town kept the land untouched for fifteen years; the June 25 quake broke the *veda*. ("Un sello se rompe. Una veda se incumple.")
- **Hayashi's inscription (祇園, Gion)** keeps the vow: he did not write the true name, only the permitted epithet, giving the rite something to say.
- **Principle:** "se apacigua, no se combate" — a collective vow appeases and contains; it does not kill.
- **Strength** grows with the number of holders, the duration and the stake of each.
- **Cap147 (Contención):** the group (priests, Valais, Wardogs, Kiki, Mizuno) swears to call it only *Gion-sama*. Each holder gives a concrete renunciation (Asha does not let go of the cord, Pier does not lift anything, Oliver does not read the name, etc.). Voss builds the physical form of the vow (the recinto = *imi-chi*). The danger: the real name.

## Compounding stake (decided 2026-10-15)

When the thing staked is the same thing the vow amplifies, the gain feeds the stake and the vow compounds: the more is held, the more is staked, the more is gained. Bounded by the holder's body: the surplus beyond what the body can release overflows as pressure (rooms narrowing, water trembling in glasses at a distance — Ji-ho's effect), and that pressure draws attention. Breaking such a vow costs in proportion to the accumulated gain.

## Rules

1. Declared aloud, with intent; cannot be forced.
2. The substrate enforces, not a person.
3. The gain grows with what is staked: specificity, cost, risk, number of holders, duration.
4. Breaking it produces a backlash proportional to the gain.
5. Does not replace training or the physical limit (Park's muscular capacity, `system/strength.md`).
6. AMAT does not know Votos as a system; the Portadores' tradition does.

## Breaking a collective vow — gradation

Backlash is shared among the holders in proportion to what each staked, with an extra share on whoever breaks it.

| Level | Cause | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Said unknowingly, without knowing it was the name | The recinto trembles; the group pays a fraction. No lasting mark |
| 2 | Said knowingly | Full break. Every renunciation reverses (Asha's palms bleed again, Pier's weight returns, Voss's recinto stops being his). The contained thing wakes fully ("decirlo lo despierta", Cap136) and turns toward the speaker. **The speaker receives the *kegare***: becomes walking forbidden ground — animals avoid or follow them, rites fail near them, what they touch rots a little, those who love them feel an unexplained cold. Only a real *harae*, performed by someone who knows how, cleans it |
| 3 | A vow sworn with one's life | Death. Not used in Cap147 |

Variants kept in reserve: silence (the speaker loses voice and their own name in others' mouths); a one-night *kegare* cleaned by Kamiya's *harae*.

## Hunger as the vow-less power

María Lionza's Hunger ("sin restricción, sin cálculo") is a power that swears nothing, so it takes without terms and cannot be bargained with. Ji-ho's curse is the same logic: the mark returns whatever Odem is given.

## Future power-ups (hooks, not decided)

Any character can in principle swear a vow that formalises a habit or a limit they already have: Oliver (a restriction on copying), Riku (discharge only by contact), Kiki, Voss. Each needs a specific stake, a holder and a cost. Do not hand out vows freely: each must have been earned by a prior limit or sacrifice.

## Primer ejemplo en pantalla de ruptura de un voto colectivo (Cap146-147, 2026-10-15)

<!-- canonical -->
- **El voto:** la veda del *imi-chi* de Kuji (quince años de no pronunciar ni escribir el nombre de lo que duerme), sostenida por la comunidad de AMAT-JP y los sacerdotes; su forma física esa noche es la *shimenawa* con *shide* y *suzu*. El epíteto *Gion-sama* es el asa permitida.
- **La ruptura (nivel 2):** Mizuno dice *Susanoo* (por reflejo de la oración de Gion y por deducción, empujado por la voz de Hayashi que usa la criatura). Efectos: cuerda floja, campanillas mudas, *shide* ardiendo, Tamura sin voz, las llagas de Asha abiertas, la pared de Voss sin obedecer un segundo; **el *kegare* cae sobre Mizuno** (ceniza gris pegada, las moscas se apartan a dos metros) y no hay quien lo limpie.
- **El nombre da forma:** al decirlo, la criatura gana bordes y cristaliza en el joven de pelo blanco.
- **Dentro de un rito el coste es menor:** Kamiya dice el nombre en el *okuri* de Cap147 "a cara descubierta"; paga con ceniza en las muñecas y se limpia en el mar. El *okuri* solo despide a los nombrados; no detiene lo demás.

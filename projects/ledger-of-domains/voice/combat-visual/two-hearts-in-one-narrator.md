# Two hearts in one narrator

- Write: one narrator paragraph that holds both fighters' states in opposition across a hinge ("while, for the first time in a thousand years, the other... feels tension"): the winner's joy, the other's strain, split into short boxes spread over two images. The contrast is the information.
- The narrator can name the replacement's intent in one line ("running toward near-certain death") while the victor's voice stays warm.
- Avoid: explaining either feeling; naming the theme (`themes/writing-rules.md`); more than once per fight.

# False victory, late bill

- Write: a win called by onlookers is overturned not by action but by a silent image and the victor's account; the reader rebuilds what was not seen. Keep the call and the reversal on separate beats.
- Avoid: showing the reversal in the same beat as the call; a reversal the earlier beats did not leave room for.

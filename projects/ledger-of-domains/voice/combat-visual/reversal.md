# Reversal with the opponent's own move

- Write: a short inventory under pressure (options considered, each dropped for a reason), the choice the enemy has not seen from you, then the return: his own pattern appears on his body and he recognizes it before he understands it. The one who returns it pays the cost on the page. If the enabling conditions were paid for in advance (a vow, `system/votos.md`, only if the scene already has one), the trigger is a flat line, the body knows before the mind, and the stake was shown earlier.
- The inventory can be five short numbered lines, then the question of what the move requires, then an elimination, then the choice the enemy has not seen.
- Avoid: explaining the move or its conditions in the moment; a free, clean return; handing out vows freely.

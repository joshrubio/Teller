# Melee, many bodies

- Write: fix the map first (who is inside the ring, who outside, which lanes are open), then move one flow through it. Small and fast pass through blind angles of large and slow; the large one's back is the opening.
- Two attackers on separate lines (level and from above): the defender's answer to one is the opening for the other.
- Two allies striking in alternation: one blow per line, one side then the other.
- Three bodies in one stack of blows with the protagonist as the hinge; the hole with a human silhouette and a tiny body at its far end is the size of the blow.
- Avoid: six simultaneous actions. One chain, each cut handing off to the next.

# Herd, then strike

- Write: the first move is not an attack but a rearrangement: it changes who stands where (forcing them into a corridor, a corner, a line, onto one side of the terrain) with ordinary means (a shove, a thrown object, a feint, the geometry of the place). The second move lands on the new arrangement. The first can be announced in a short line split around the face ("maximum... output"); the second can be named over the rubble with an exact elapsed time ("in forty-one seconds...").
- Avoid: a pull, a field or a gravity that gathers by itself (no mechanism of ours supports it); showing the second before the first has rearranged; explaining the rearrangement.

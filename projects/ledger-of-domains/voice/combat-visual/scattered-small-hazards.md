# Scattered small hazards

- Write: many small, same-shaped hazards laid across the route; the mover threads through them and the repeated sound is the count. The field thins as they go off, so the route is read from which ones are missing. The controller is big and close in the foreground; the target is small at the far end: the distance between the two bodies is the tension.
- The hazards can be a swarm of small, identical bodies that works as a curtain; one blow clears one with the same soft sound each time, and the quip is aimed at the one hidden behind.
- Avoid: describing each hazard; explaining what it does; a run with no body on the route.

# Strange choice, late reason

- Write: the protagonist does something that looks like a mistake (drops a guard, stops protecting or recovering himself); the onlookers or the enemy register it in short lines ("why..."), and the question stands for a beat or two. Then the resource logic in three plain facts, said by someone watching; then the reveal that reframes the omission (he was spending it where it mattered). Name the move alone on a line before its effect; the payoff blow is one word. The body keeps the count: marks pile up one place per beat and close without announcement.
- The payoff can be a restart, not a finish: the choice is what resets the contest to even.
- The gamble can be confessed afterward in a light tone, and the reason given by someone else.
- Avoid: explaining at once; a reveal the earlier beat did not plant; a medical report of the wounds.

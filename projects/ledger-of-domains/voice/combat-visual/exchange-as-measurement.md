# Exchange as measurement

- Write: the first clash as a probe, then a chain of short blows (one per line), then what contact told you: how the body gives, how fast it answers, where it hurts. At most one tactile comparison. End on the risk the information carries.
- Mid-fight, each side can read the other in one short line (what he is doing, what that says); the two lines reveal different things. A chain of short thoughts, one attribute each (toughness, speed, output), is enough, and it ends on a risk.
- Avoid: explaining mechanisms; capabilities arrive through contact, not as a briefing.

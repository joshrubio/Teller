# Matched commitment

- Write: one side commits its maximum; the other answers at that same instant (one cry of disbelief, one smile). The answer does not hit back: it changes what the other's effort can reach, read through repeated blows that stop landing. Rules are said once, flatly: a maximum commitment trades against something (the wider, the weaker); doing one thing blocks another; the exception is stated once. When the strongest advantage of one side is switched off, the fight returns to bodies (`close-quarters.md`). Close the beat with one line that restarts it ("again").
- Avoid: explaining the mechanism of either side; the cost must be seen first (`strange-choice-late-reason.md`). Take only the strategy of the reference, never its concepts.

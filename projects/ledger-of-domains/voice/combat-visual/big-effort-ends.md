# Big effort ends

- Write: the break said in two narrator lines split by an ellipsis ("...his effort... collapses"). Both outcomes stated in two parallel lines (one broke the other's from outside; the other hurt him until he could not hold his). The body takes the cuts but the damage reads "superficial" because of a basic skill from long ago, said in one dry line ("not bad, right?"). The breadth of an effort can be shown as a sweep over a wide view with a sound line and no bodies. How long it lasted is information: plant a counter earlier, or the gap reads as a gag. One image of the cost with a smile (blood, no comment) closes the beat.
- Pay off an early threshold with an exact time: onlookers say "three minutes" beforehand, then the narrator says "two minutes forty" over the wide image of the collapse.
- The narrator can open the payoff with the exact elapsed time as a fragment ("in forty-one seconds...") and complete it over the rubble.
- Avoid: a medical report; explaining the old skill.

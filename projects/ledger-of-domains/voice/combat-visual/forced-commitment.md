# Forced commitment

- Write: end on the line "I have no other option", said twice (once at the start of the last thought, once after a smile). The repetition does not inform; it binds him to what comes. Close on the smile, with no comment, and the next beat is the move he named.
- Avoid: showing the move before the chapter break; explaining it.

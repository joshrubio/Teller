# Pass and answer (vertical)

- Write: the answer as what it leaves (cut air, hair arriving late, one sound), not the striking limb or weapon; the airborne body carries its own momentum past the answer. The miss is the setup: the answer is born from the space the blow left empty. The near threat gets the biggest, most concrete sentence; the one avoiding it is small and above. Show the charged state before the strike lands.
- Avoid: showing the instrument and the arc together; one clean beat, not a chain.

# Signature from the wrong body

- Write: the principal's known move arrives from the support, from far away; the onlookers read it in a chain of short questions (weakening? / like him? / from a distance? / did the support do it?), each adding a suspicion until the last names the source. Say it as questions, not as an explanation.
- Avoid: explaining how the support has the move; naming the reference's mechanism.

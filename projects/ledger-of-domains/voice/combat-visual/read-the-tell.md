# Read the tell

- Write: every big move has a visible precursor (a gathering, a held breath, the first word of a recital); the narrator says there always is one, and the one who reads it picks which blow it is. The enemy's reading can be right and still be the bait.
- Avoid: naming the reference's mechanism; a tell the earlier beats did not plant.

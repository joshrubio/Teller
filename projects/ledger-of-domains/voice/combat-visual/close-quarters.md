# Close quarters

- Write: when bodies touch, drop the distances; the map is contact points (whose collar, whose wrist, where the heads are). One blow per line, the physical effect as the close. When everything happens in seconds, a blow and its answer can share one sentence (the same body at two instants). Under damage the POV's inner voice shrinks to commands, and the stake is said once, plainly; the cost shows on the body before it is spoken. Recovering mid-fight (our own, Presión Inversa, `world/odem.md`) is a command to the body and a visible cost, never a reset.
- In the air, two bodies on one diagonal, the point of contact (knee, elbow) as the hinge of the exchange.
- Hand-fighting (palm parries, finger slaps) can be the whole exchange; the faces show who read what. A grip can turn the architecture into the weapon (door, panel): the grab is the cause, the structure is the blow.
- After a pivot, the same bodies can appear several times in one beat, only the distance changing; one blow and its answer can share a sentence; a clean blow can be answered by one dry refusal, not a speech. Show the charge on a limb before it strikes (thin lines like heat haze), never a wound report.
- The same body at two instants split by a diagonal can carry a fast, long-range exchange, not only a short one.
- A thin cut through a limb mid-sequence changes the rhythm without stopping the exchange.
- The same body twice in one frame (striking and falling) with one sound marking the change of direction; a blow on the face that sends a hand to the nose.
- A taunt that reduces the opponent to one resource ("a one-trick pony") while striking.
- Avoid: wide choreography and cardinal directions at zero distance; a long tactical monologue under fire (`combat.md` §8); recovering for free.

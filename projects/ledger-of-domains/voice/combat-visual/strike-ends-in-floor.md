# Strike that ends in the floor

- Write: the mark shows where the target was; debris falls where the opponent went. Scale force by what breaks around it (a door folds, glass sprays up and out); the body below gives like the material does, written with the same specificity.
- Cracks radiating from the point of the fall give the force without a number.
- Avoid: a floor burst every exchange (`combat.md` §5).

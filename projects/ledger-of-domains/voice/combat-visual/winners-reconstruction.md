# Winner's reconstruction

- Write: the winner tells the fallen how he won, in plain staggered boxes, one idea each; his method was patience (he waited until an ally learned what he could not do alone). Close on one short line of respect over a bloodied smile.
- Avoid: gloating; explaining the mechanism of the technique.

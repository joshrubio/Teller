# Hidden payer

- Write: the move that looked free was paid for by someone the reader did not expect (often the one who seemed to profit). Tell it as a silent reconstruction: a run of plain narrator lines, one idea each, staggered in order (what he had done before, what each side aimed at, what cancelled, how many times, who bore the cost), then cut the last sentence before it names the payer ("and the one who really bore this was..."). Over it, one image with no face. The reader believed the blow clean; the page costs it back.
- Avoid: explaining the mechanism of either side; a payer the earlier beats did not plant; the reference's terms.

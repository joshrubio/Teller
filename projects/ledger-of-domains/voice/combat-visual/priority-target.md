# Priority target

- Write: say the order of targets in one line with its reason ("it would be a pain if it healed him, so the priority is to crush the support"). The one who sustains the enemy goes first; the enemy himself waits.
- Avoid: attacking the principal first and explaining later; a priority with no cost.

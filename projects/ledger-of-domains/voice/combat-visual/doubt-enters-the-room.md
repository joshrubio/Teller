# Doubt enters the room

- Write: the first thought of losing arrives in three beats. The onlookers note something measurable (wounds close slower, and the same for the enemy), the narrator says in staggered plain boxes that a thought nobody thought possible has crossed their minds, and the protagonist registers it with one short line ("how long since I felt this?"). Keep any emotion in the body (a smile he cannot stop, a closing hand) and do not name the theme (`themes/writing-rules.md`).
- Avoid: explaining the meaning of the feeling; a speech about solitude or strength.

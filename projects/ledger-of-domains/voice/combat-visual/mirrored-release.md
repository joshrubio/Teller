# Mirrored release

- Write: the eyes first (one physical detail each), one short line for why now, then two releases built the same way, meeting at one point between them; the bodies small at the edges. The symmetry is the information.
- When two efforts of equal strength meet, the stalemate is one dark point at the centre with debris thrown outward; it breaks by one thin line, not by more force, and the verdict follows.
- The narrator can state the handicap and the reason for firing first; a reading of the other's gaze goes over the faces, and the release itself carries no text.
- Avoid: explaining both systems; give the release a physical mechanism, not a light show.

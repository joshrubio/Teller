# Collision on a held line

- Write: faces first (one physical detail each), then the contact. The defender's line (blade, forearm, barrier) is fixed; the attack arrives on a diagonal and is what gives. A third body at the edge is a pending threat, not a spectator. The line can fail audibly (a creak) and the defender's body (eyes, jaw, a flinch) carries the force.
- When both hold the line, the lock itself is the beat: two small bodies, one horizontal line between them, feet planted.
- Avoid: describing both fighters' full movement; the held line carries the beat.

# Cause here, effect there

- Write: the cause up close (one physical detail), then wide on the effect far away, as one point of impact in a large space, a chain of impacts spaced across it, or a cone widening along the line of a gesture. The distance and speed are read from what breaks and from the gaps between impacts, not stated. The gesture is the pointer: write its line to the far target; the target is small at the end of it and its reaction waits one beat. A beat of pure motion with no body in it can sit between cause and effect. The effect can also be a straight scar through the space instead of a cone; what is left untouched at its edges is the information. A short command shouted alone can be the cut to the wide shot of the whole ground, the accumulated damage shown at once. A one-line reaction from a distant watcher can mark the stakes, always as a scene break, never inside the POV chain (`combat.md` §1). The path of a thrown body is read from the structures it breaks one after another; the pursuer is already moving when it lands.
- The target can be a black silhouette inside a storm of strokes; the damage is the density, not the list.
- A repeated sound over a wide shot can mark the gaps between impacts without describing them.
- A diagonal scar across a skyline, with smoke columns and two small bodies on a rooftop, is the proof of the blow without showing it; the same line can cross the body (see `cost-on-the-body-once.md`).
- A long curved arc across a facade repeated in small panels, with a repeated sound, is the signature of a blow you never see.
- Avoid: describing the whole space; only the impact matters.

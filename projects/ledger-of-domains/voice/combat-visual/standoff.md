# Standoff

- Write: the full-body stance first (feet, weight, what the hands carry), then one taunt aimed at the sore point and one answer that refuses it. A face or voice the enemy borrows makes the hit costly; the refusal is said once, flatly. End on the first movement, not on a speech.
- When the odds worsen, answer with one dry remark about the other's appearance, not about the fight.
- One approving word from the enemy ("lovely") over a half-lit face is a threat; a taunt that names the enemy as a misfit ("you don't fit this group") can be said while striking.
- Avoid: a long monologue; explaining the relationship behind the face.

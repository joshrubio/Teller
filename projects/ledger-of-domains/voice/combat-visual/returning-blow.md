# Returning blow

- Write: a blow that seems spent is announced as "not yet" in one short line; later it arrives from behind through the geometry of the place (a wall, a corner, the building itself). The reveal is a plain floor plan or a path line; two onlooker lines say "he used the structure" and "he planned it from the start". The enemy gets one line of disbelief ("from behind?").
- Avoid: a blow whose route was not plantable; explaining its delay.

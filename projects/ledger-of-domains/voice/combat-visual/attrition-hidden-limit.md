# Attrition on a hidden limit

- Write: the blows are aimed at something other than the body; the defender reads that aim in short lines, and the cost is lost control, not damage.
- The target's own inner lines can name the hidden aim and its price in two beats (what they are doing, what is really at risk) before the blow lands.
- Avoid: explaining the mechanism; the cost must be specific (what he can no longer hold).

# Deny the window

- Write: the one who owns the strongest blow spends the fight making sure the other never gets a gap to fire his; one dry inner line ("all I have to do is deny him an opening... this is going well"). The reader sees the pressure as the means, not the end.
- Avoid: showing the finisher; explaining why the gap matters (plant it earlier).

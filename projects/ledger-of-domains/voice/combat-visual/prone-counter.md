# Prone counter

- Write: the one who seems down is where he wants to be; one calm detail first (a smile, a raised hand), then the smallest gesture and the largest result. The reader's belief about who has the advantage flips on that gesture.
- Avoid: showing the effort of the counter; the disproportion is the information.

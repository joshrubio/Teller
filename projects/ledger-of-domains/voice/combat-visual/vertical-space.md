# Vertical space

- Write: name the surface and the fall line (a facade, balconies as footholds, which way the body drops); the fighters are small against the building. The damage appears on the facade after they pass (windows shattering in a column, cuts left behind). A wide shot with two small bodies between towers gives the scale. Inside a structure, rotate the map and say which wall is now the floor; doors and frames give the measure.
- When the route is altered as you move, show it by what the mover has to leap, duck or go around; the face stays grim.
- A tilted camera can lay both bodies on their sides while one rises and the other falls; say which wall is now the floor.
- Avoid: forgetting gravity; a long fall needs a landing.

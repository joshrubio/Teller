# False toolbox

- Write: the onlookers debate what options exist; one short line from the protagonist says the other believes he only has one. Later he uses the one nobody counted. The reader knows there is a hidden option, not which, nor when. The enemy's taunt ("lost your calm?") can be the bait: it is said while the blow is still in flight and is the line that leaves him undefended.
- The enemy has hidden options too: a posture that should not be his gives away a borrowed move, with two onlooker lines (recognition; a name with a question mark) and the hit as a thin line, not a flash.
- Avoid: showing the option early; explaining the rule; the reference's names.

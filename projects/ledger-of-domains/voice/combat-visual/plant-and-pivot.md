# Plant and pivot

- Write: the plant is where the weight stops; torso follows the leg, arm last. Give the sweep a radius: what is inside, what is outside, which way dodges. Carry the approach's speed into the rotation.
- Avoid: spinning for style. A pivot spends position; show the exposed back afterwards.

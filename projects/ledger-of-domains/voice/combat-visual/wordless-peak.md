# Wordless peak

- Write: at the highest speed, drop the narrator, the thought lines and the dialogue; verbs, debris and sounds carry the beat. One fixed object crosses several cuts (a chunk of rubble the POV sees again on turning). A sound repeated three or four times works as a metronome over a wide shot where the bodies are small. The faces alternate in a mirror (a wide grin, a wide eye) and say who leads. The space is broken as they pass: the route and the damage are the same.
- Three equal impact sounds in a row (a metronome) can close a blast before any voice.
- A white double page with only a radial burst and a skyline in blocks; the wreck that follows, with the loser sunk into a wall, is the measure of the effort.
- Avoid: explaining the speed; a long run with no face beat; a sound label standing in for a verb.
